📺 The captain's deck

Put the pond view on the meeting-room screen, and share the colleague link so everyone can join from their phones.

The pond is for light-hearted check-ins, not confidential feedback. Everyone with a room link can view its boats. No sign-in or password is required.

🌟 How it works

  1. Open the big-screen pond link on the projector.
  2. Share the colleague link (or turn it into a QR code).
  3. Colleagues choose their animal, hobby, snack, boat colour and mood.
  4. The boats appear together on your screen automatically!

Tip: Use a different room code in the URL for another team or meeting.
